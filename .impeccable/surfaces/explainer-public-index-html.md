# Research explainer

Mode: Read. Audience: researchers and visitors new to language models. Build directly in code, as the user selected. Visual decisions are delegated by the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An interactive poster makes the full experiment visible as five connected, clickable stages. Explanation, progress and results share one page.

OWN-WORLD: Ice blue and white surfaces, deep navy type, cobalt active controls, teal verified facts and amber planned settings. Atkinson Hyperlegible lettering, precise equation rows, generous reading space and a compact navigation rail.

STORY: Visitors see the original and compact forms, trace five experimental stages, open deeper explanations, then inspect what has actually been completed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A visual infographic begins immediately under the research question, with connected figures in the main column and the nested data groups alongside. Longer explanations stay hidden. Clicking a figure opens a right-hand drawer on desktop or a bottom sheet on mobile, with three detail levels. Status is explicit but secondary.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3, an interactive mathematics museum exhibit. Seed e98b4309. Other candidates: laboratory notebook, annotated tutorial, census sheet, protocol manual, project wall and paper marginalia. Carbon-column and folded-paper challengers lose clarity for text-heavy reading; their discipline raises the continuity of the experiment and before/after registration. Exposure sheets and cutting rails lose beginner identification; their discipline raises the clarity of active states. The monochrome reference is competitive in clarity but less identifiable to this multilingual experiment. The quarterly loses clarity for interactive controls; its discipline raises the reading measure and typography. Mobbin's Turo lesson reinforces progressive disclosure; Codecademy reinforces adjacent example and explanation.

SIGNATURE: Clicking an infographic figure reveals its explanation in a native dialog with keyboard focus and Escape dismissal. A desktop drawer slides from the side; a mobile sheet rises from the bottom. The idea, visual example and method details expose progressively deeper information. Reduced motion disables movement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
